# Acknowledgements

The Urban Heat Mitigation Platform would not have been possible without the
generous support of the following organizations, projects, and individuals.

## Satellite Data Providers

- **Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)** — For providing high-resolution
  satellite imagery and environmental data through the Bhuvan platform, which
  powers our land surface analysis for the Indian subcontinent.

- **National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)** — For the Landsat
  program and EarthData infrastructure, which have provided free and open access
  to the longest continuous space-based record of Earth's land surface. The
  Landsat 8 and 9 missions are the backbone of our LST retrieval and spectral
  index computations.

- **European Space Agency (ESA)** — For the Copernicus Sentinel-2 mission,
  delivering high-resolution multispectral imagery at a global scale with rapid
  revisit times, enabling near-real-time urban heat monitoring.

- **United States Geological Survey (USGS)** — For maintaining the EarthExplorer
  data portal and the Landsat archive with consistent calibration and
  documentation.

## Hackathon

- **Bharat Antariksh Hackathon** — For bringing together the brightest minds in
  space technology, artificial intelligence, and sustainable urban development.
  This platform was conceived and built during this extraordinary event.

- **Organizers and Mentors** — For their tireless efforts in coordinating the
  hackathon, providing technical guidance, and creating an environment where
  innovation thrives.
